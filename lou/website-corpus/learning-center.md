# Learning Center

Welcome to the Learning Center for the Neuromine data portal! Here, you’ll find a collection of instructional videos designed to help you navigate through the various features and tools of the Neuromine portal and office hours information and links. Each image below is a link to a YouTube video that provides step-by-step guidance on specific functions such as searching for data, downloading data, ordering iPSC lines, and gaining access to the portal. These resources are aimed at enhancing your experience and efficiency when using the Neuromine portal for your research needs.

## Learning Videos

### Data Search Tool

Welcome to our tutorial on how to effectively use the Neuromine search and filter tool. In this video, we’ll guide you through the process of locating and refining data searches to help you maximize your research efficiency with Neuromine.

### Accessing Data

Welcome to our instructional video on how to gain access to data within the Neuromine portal. This video will guide you through the steps required to secure permissions and unlock the full suite of data resources available, ensuring you have the access needed for your scientific inquiries.

### GUI Data Download Tool

Welcome to our quick guide on using the Neuromine Data Download Tool. In this video, we’ll show you step-by-step how to efficiently download the data you need efficiently, ensuring you have all the resources at your fingertips for your research or project.

### CLI Data Download Tool

Welcome to our tutorial on the Neuromine Data Download Tool using a command line interface. We’ll demonstrate how to use the CLI to download data directly to your local server, ensuring you efficiently access all the necessary resources for your projects or research.

### Ordering iPSC Lines

Hello and welcome to our tutorial on how to order iPSC lines using the Neuromine portal. In this video, we’ll walk you through each step of the ordering process, using the portal’s intuitive tools to ensure you can easily access the specific iPSC lines you need for your research.

### Transfer Data to ADDI

Welcome to our tutorial on how to transfer data for custom cohorts from the Neuromine portal to an ADDI workspace. In this video, we’ll guide you through each step of the process, showing you how to use Neuromine’s intuitive tools to define your cohort, select relevant datasets, and seamlessly move the data into your secure ADDI environment for analysis and collaboration.
