# Datasets

##### Welcome to the Neuromine Data Portal

The Neuromine data portal is designed to make finding and accessing data simple. Once you are an approved user, the tools and data available include an interactive search and filter tool, data visualization tools, de-identified clinical and OMIC data from over 2700 participants.

Steps to gaining access:

1. Register with the portal by navigating to the Sign In button. New users will need to provide name and email address for communication from Neuromine. If you are already registered, proceed to step two.
2. Once signed into the portal, you will see a "Request Access" button. Click the button to begin the approval process. Please note that access will be granted to one email for the account. It is recommended that you use a group email for this purpose.
3. You will be directed to an online form to provide basic personal, institutional, and collaborator information, provide a data use research proposal, and agree to data use terms.
4. Sign an online Data Use Agreement (DUA).

   **Please note we require specific signatures on the DUA.**

   If you are a graduate student, postdoc, scientist, or research associate, you must have your supervising PI, Director, or above co-sign the DUA. Please include the title and level of this person in your request using the Collaborator section. If you are a Principal Investigator (PI), Director, or above, you may be the Data Requestor and do not need to list another PI or Director as a collaborator.

   A Business Official also needs to sign on behalf of your associated entity. Please contact your contracts office or PI to identify the correct person for this role. Answer ALS does not know the policies of each Institution and therefore suggests you ensure the right person is identified for this role. It is entirely up to the data requestor to determine who should sign for the University, Institution, or Company.

   If you have collaborators that are part of your Institution, they may be added as a collaborator on this request. If your collaborators are outside your Institution, they will need to request separate access.

Once approved, you will have access to all the Neuromine data and tools.

1. Search and filter based on clinical metadata, WGS variants, data types and cell lines.
2. Download OMIC and Clinical metadata based on search results.
3. Request cell lines based on search results.
4. Request biosamples (coming soon!).
5. Visualize cohort data using heatmap, box, volcano and PCA plots.
