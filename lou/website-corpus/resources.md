## Resources

Below are descriptions and links to external sites, documents, and tools to help you with your research efforts.

The AD Workbench, developed by the Alzheimer’s Disease Data Initiative (ADDI), is a secure cloud-based platform that enables researchers to analyze data collaboratively in a controlled environment. Through the Workbench, users can access curated datasets and powerful analysis tools without needing to download large files locally. Data from Answer ALS are available within this platform for approved users. Simply search for your desired cohort and hit Next to launch your session in the AD Workbench environment.

The Cedars-Sinai Biomanufacturing Center is a state-of-the-art facility specializing in producing high-quality cellular products for clinical use. One of its key offerings is the production and distribution of induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) lines, which are available for purchase and can be used in various research applications. The Answer ALS lines used to generate the data available on the portal is available for purchase through the center. Simply search for your desired cohort and hit Next to take your selection over to the Cedars site for purchase.

The Neurological Clinical Research Institute (NCRI) at Mass General Hospital is a leading center for clinical research in neurological diseases, including ALS. As part of its commitment to open science, NCRI provides access to high-quality biosamples, including plasma, serum, and cerebrospinal fluid, collected from Answer ALS participants. These samples are available for qualified researchers and can be requested directly through the portal. Simply search for your desired cohort and follow the link to the left to proceed with your request via the NCRI biosample request system.

Many Answer ALS control lines are available through the California Institute for Regenerative Medicine (CIRM) iPSC Repository. The CIRM repository is a comprehensive resource offering a wide selection of high-quality induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) lines derived from diverse donor populations for use in research and clinical applications. These iPSC lines are publicly accessible and can support studies in various fields, including regenerative medicine and disease modeling. Researchers can easily browse the available lines and place orders through the repository’s online portal, facilitating access to this valuable resource for advancing stem cell research.

The Answer ALS visualization tools go beyond graphic metadata visualization applications for searching and finding specific cohort or cell line information. We also have tools to give insight into the RNA counts from a selected cohort. Use the search and filter tool to find your cohort of interest and select the “next” button at the bottom right of the search page. From there, select “Visualize Data” to take your selected participants to view in a heatmap, boxplots, and more.

Visit the Answer ALS GitHub repository to join the community of researchers committed to advancing our understanding of ALS. Here, you’ll find a collaborative platform where researchers can share scripts and code with each other and access valuable resources shared by others. The Answer, ALS GitHub repository, is an open-source platform that encourages sharing knowledge and resources, making it a powerful tool for accelerating research in this critical area.

Our document library contains downloadable files covering topics such as data management, process optimization, and protocols, all specifically tailored to the Answer ALS data. Whether you’re a seasoned researcher or just starting out, our easy-to-navigate interface makes finding and downloading the files you need easy.
