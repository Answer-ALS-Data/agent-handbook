## Purpose

This document is a training resource for an LLM-based conversational assistant deployed on the Neuromine Data Portal. It serves as the single authoritative reference for how users get help, what support channels exist, what to expect when contacting the team, and how publication and citation requirements work.

This document covers: the official support channel, response and approval timelines, office hours schedule and format, publication policy and citation guidance, and how to report issues or provide feedback.

This document does not cover: portal navigation (see Portal Map and Naming Guide), login and account troubleshooting (see Account and Identity FAQ), or the end-to-end data access request process (see Access and Approvals Handbook).

## Section 1: Contacting Support

*This section covers the most common support question users ask: how to reach the Neuromine team. It establishes the single canonical contact channel and sets expectations for what kinds of questions the support team handles.*

---

Q: How do I contact the Neuromine support team?

Context: *This is one of the most frequently asked questions in the portal chatbot. Users ask this in many forms — "contact support," "who do I email," "how do I reach someone." In the past, the assistant gave inconsistent answers, sometimes listing multiple email addresses or naming individual staff members. There is one canonical support channel.*

A: The Neuromine Data Portal support team can be reached by email at **data@answerals.org**. This is the single point of contact for all support inquiries, including data access questions, account issues, technical problems, feedback, and general questions about the portal or its datasets.

Note: *Do not provide any other email addresses, phone numbers, or individual staff names as contact options. All support requests should be directed to data@answerals.org regardless of the topic. The support team handles internal routing.*

---

Q: Is there a phone number I can call for support?

Context: *Users occasionally ask for a phone number or live chat option. No phone support line exists. The chatbot assistant and email are the available support channels.*

A: There is no phone number for Neuromine support. All support is handled by email at **data@answerals.org**. For live interaction, the team holds weekly office hours every Monday — see the Office Hours section of this document for details.

---

Q: What kinds of questions can I send to the support team?

Context: *Users sometimes hesitate about whether their question is "appropriate" for the support email, or they wonder if different types of issues go to different channels. Everything goes to the same place.*

A: You can contact **data@answerals.org** for any question related to the Neuromine Data Portal, including:

- Data access requests and DUA questions
- Account or login issues
- Technical problems with the portal or download tool
- Questions about available datasets or data formats
- Feedback or suggestions about the portal
- Bug reports
- Publication and citation questions

There is no need to determine the right contact for your type of question — the support team will route your inquiry to the appropriate person.

---

Q: How quickly will I hear back from support?

Context: *Users frequently ask about response times, especially when waiting on access requests. It is important to distinguish between the initial support response time and the full DUA approval timeline, which are very different. This question covers general support response time. DUA approval timelines are covered in Section 2.*

A: The support team typically responds to inquiries within **one week**. Note that this refers to the initial response to your message — if your question involves a data access request or DUA approval, the full approval process takes longer due to institutional contracts, signatures, and compliance requirements. See Section 2 of this document for details on access request timelines.

## Section 2: Access Request Timelines

*This section addresses one of the most common sources of user confusion: the difference between receiving a response from the support team and receiving final approval on a data access request. Users frequently contact the chatbot asking why their application is still pending after weeks or months.*

---

Q: How long does it take to get a response on my access request?

Context: *Users often conflate "response" with "approval." The support team responds to inquiries within about a week, but the full DUA approval process is a separate, longer timeline. This question addresses initial response time specifically in the context of access requests.*

A: The support team will respond to your access request inquiry within approximately **one week**. However, receiving a response does not mean your request has been fully approved — the response confirms your request has been received and is being processed. The full approval process involves additional steps and takes longer. See the next entry for details on approval timelines.

---

Q: My access request has been pending for weeks. Is that normal?

Context: *This is a frequent concern. Users submit their Data Access Request and DUA, then wait — sometimes for weeks or months — without understanding why. The approval process involves institutional contracts, signatures, and compliance with IRB standards, all of which take time.*

A: Yes. It is normal for the full approval process to take several weeks or longer, including waits of a month or more. A multi-week wait — including five weeks, six weeks, or longer — is within the expected range and is not by itself a sign that something is wrong with your application. Approving a data access request involves more than a simple review — it requires:

- Execution of a Data Use Agreement (DUA) between your institution and the data providers
- Institutional signatures, often from an authorized business official at your organization
- Compliance review to meet IRB and regulatory standards
- Coordination between multiple parties

These steps take time, and the timeline depends in part on how quickly your institution and signatories respond. Do not characterize a multi-week wait as "longer than typical" — several weeks is typical for the full approval process.

If your request has been pending and you have not heard back, **reach out to the support team at data@answerals.org**. Following up lets the team know you are engaged and ready to move forward, which helps them prioritize your request among the many they receive.

---

Q: How can I help my access request move faster?

Context: *Users sometimes ask what they can do to speed things up. The most actionable advice is to follow up with the support team. Proactive communication helps ensure the request stays on track.*

A: The best thing you can do is **contact the support team at data@answerals.org** to follow up on your request. The team manages a high volume of access requests, and following up helps ensure yours is moving through the process. The support team can let you know if anything is needed on your end to keep things progressing.

Note: *Do not speculate about reasons for delays or comment on the volume of requests in a way that implies some applicants are deprioritized. Simply direct the user to follow up with support.*

## Section 3: Office Hours

*This section provides operational details for the Neuromine Data Portal's weekly office hours. Users frequently ask about office hours and the chatbot previously failed to answer. The Portal Map and Naming Guide covers where to find office hours information on the portal (Learning Center tab); this section covers the schedule, format, and how to participate.*

---

Q: When are the Neuromine office hours?

Context: *This is a common question that the chatbot previously could not answer, responding with "I couldn't find specific information about office hours." The schedule is consistent and should always be provided.*

A: Neuromine office hours are held **every Monday from 10:00 to 11:00 AM PT (1:00 to 2:00 PM ET)** via Microsoft Teams.

---

Q: What topics are covered in office hours?

Context: *Users want to know whether their question is appropriate for office hours or whether they should email support instead. Office hours cover a broad range of topics related to using the portal and working with the data.*

A: Office hours cover a wide range of topics, including:

- Data quality questions
- Technical support and troubleshooting
- Download tool usage
- Clinical and OMIC dataset questions
- Data structure and metadata

If you have a question about using the portal or working with the data, office hours are a good place to get live help.

---

Q: How do I join office hours?

Context: *Users want to know the logistics — how to connect, whether they need a link, and whether they should prepare anything in advance.*

A: Office hours are held via **Microsoft Teams**. To get the meeting link or to submit questions in advance, contact the support team at **data@answerals.org**.

You can also find office hours information on the portal under the **Learning Center** tab.

---

Q: Do I need an appointment or registration for office hours?

Context: *Some users ask whether office hours require registration, or confuse them with scheduled demos. There is no registration process — office hours are an open recurring session.*

A: No registration is required. Office hours are an open session held every Monday. Just join via Microsoft Teams at the scheduled time. If you need the meeting link, contact **data@answerals.org**.

Note: *There is no separate demo or live event registration process. If a user asks about demos or live walkthroughs, direct them to office hours as the venue for live support. Tutorial videos are also available on the Learning Center tab.*

## Section 4: Publications and Citation

*This section covers the publication policy, citation and acknowledgement requirements, and where to find the publications list. Users have asked the chatbot for publications lists and citation guidance, and the chatbot previously returned empty responses. The Portal Map and Naming Guide covers navigation to the Publications tab; this section covers what the policy says and how to comply with it.*

---

Q: Where can I find publications that used Answer ALS or Neuromine data?

Context: *Users ask for a list of publications and the chatbot previously failed to return any results. The publications list is maintained on the portal itself.*

A: A list of publications that have used Answer ALS and Neuromine data is available on the **Publications tab** of the Neuromine Data Portal. The list is updated periodically.

---

Q: What are the citation and acknowledgement requirements for using Neuromine data?

Context: *The Data Use Agreement includes specific citation and acknowledgement requirements. Users need to know these before publishing. The definitive source for citation language is the Publications tab and the DUA itself — the assistant should direct users there rather than reproducing citation text, which may be updated over time.*

A: If you use data from the Neuromine Data Portal in a publication or presentation, you are required to include specific acknowledgement and citation language. The exact requirements, including citation text for both Answer ALS and ALS TDI data, can be found on the **Publications tab** of the portal and in your **Data Use Agreement**.

You must also submit a copy of your citation to the data providers upon publication. Contact **data@answerals.org** to submit.

Note: *Do not reproduce specific citation text or acknowledgement language. Direct users to the Publications tab and their DUA as the authoritative sources.*

---

Q: Do I need to submit my manuscript for review before publishing?

Context: *The DUA requires a 30-day pre-publication review. This is an administrative review, not a scientific review or approval gate. Users need to know about this requirement early in their publication process.*

A: Yes. Under the Data Use Agreement, you must submit draft manuscripts to the data providers **30 days prior to publication** for administrative review. This is not a scientific review or approval process — it is an administrative step required by the DUA.

To submit a manuscript for review, contact **data@answerals.org**.

---

Q: Can I publish the original Answer ALS or ALS TDI data?

Context: *The DUA explicitly prohibits publishing or claiming ownership of the original datasets. Users may publish derivative analyses and results, but not the source data itself.*

A: No. The Data Use Agreement prohibits publishing, redistributing, or claiming ownership of the original datasets. You are encouraged to publish the results of your analyses and derivative works, but the underlying data from the portal may not be republished.

If you have questions about what constitutes a derivative work or whether your planned publication complies with the DUA, contact **data@answerals.org**.

## Section 5: Reporting Issues and Giving Feedback

*This section covers how users can report bugs, give feedback, or flag problems with the portal. The chatbot previously directed users to different email addresses depending on whether the issue was a bug or feedback. All reports go through the same channel.*

---

Q: How do I report a bug or problem with the portal?

Context: *Users encounter technical issues — broken links, failed downloads, display errors, login problems — and want to know where to report them. The chatbot previously gave inconsistent guidance, sometimes suggesting different channels for different issue types.*

A: Report any technical issue or bug to **data@answerals.org**. Include a description of the problem, what you were trying to do, and any error messages you received. The support team will triage and route the issue.

---

Q: How do I give feedback or make a suggestion about the portal?

Context: *Users occasionally want to suggest improvements or share general feedback rather than report a specific problem. This goes through the same support channel.*

A: Send your feedback or suggestions to **data@answerals.org**. The team welcomes input from users on how to improve the portal and its data resources.

Note: *Do not direct users to any other email address for feedback. In the past, the assistant mentioned answeralsdata@gmail.com as a feedback channel — this is no longer correct. All feedback goes to data@answerals.org.*
