# Create an SOP

## What you get

A standard operating procedure (SOP) in Word for one team process, filled into the house SOP template and written to the house rules.

## Read first

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/AGENTS.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/templates/sop-template.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/data/naming.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/lou/INDEX.md

## Steps

1. If the person did not say which process to document, ask. Ask for what goes in, what comes out, who runs it and how they check it.
2. Ask which program it is for: Answer ALS (AALS-), ALS TDI (ALSTDI-) or Neuromine (NEUROMINE-).
3. Fetch the template https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/templates/answer-als-sop-template.docx. Use the template as it is. Do not restyle it.
4. Copy the template and fill it in place as sop-template.md says: title table, first Revision History row, the five sections, one step heading per stage. Do not change styles or numbering.
5. Give every stage one Verify check and end Procedures with a final validation step. Write References "None." if nothing outside is cited.
6. Keep the budget: 700 to 1,000 words, hard cap 1,100. Cut before you go over.
7. Save the docx as <PREFIX>-SOP-NNN-V1. <Title>.docx, as naming.md says. Check that every paragraph is ASCII and count the words.
8. List each fact you could not confirm from the person, a Lou page or the handbook, such as a file name, a step order or who signs off.

## Hand back

The .docx file, its word count and the list of open items. The data owner assigns the SOP number and copies the file to the SOP folder. Never send the SOP to anyone. The person shares it.
