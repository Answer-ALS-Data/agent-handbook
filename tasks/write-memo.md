# Write a memo or one-pager

## What you get

A memo, letter or one-page summary in Word, made from the standard Answer ALS memo or one-pager template.

## Read first

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/AGENTS.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/README.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/templates/docx-templates.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/lou/INDEX.md

## Steps

1. If the person did not say who reads it, what decision or action it asks for and whether it is a memo, a letter or a one-pager, ask.
2. Fetch the template: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/templates/answer-als-memo-template.docx for a memo or letter, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Answer-ALS-Data/agent-handbook/main/design/templates/answer-als-one-pager-template.docx for a one-page summary. docx-templates.md says how to use them.
3. Use the template as it is. Do not restyle it and do not rebuild it.
4. Copy the template and replace each bracketed instruction with text, inside its word limit. Use the named styles only.
5. Take program facts and counts only as quoted from a Lou page. Put a source line naming that page under any table or number.
6. Cut any section the document does not need. A memo is at most two pages and a one-pager is one page.

## Hand back

The .docx file, saved under a new name, plus a short note of any fact you could not fill. Never send the document to anyone. The person sends it.
