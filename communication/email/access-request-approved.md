# Access request approved

## When to use

The Data Access Committee approved the request, every party signed in DocuSign and the team countersigned.
Send it to the requester once the account can see the data on the Datasets tab.

## Subject

Your Neuromine data access is ready

## Body

Hi [First name],

Your data access request for [Program] on the Neuromine Data Portal is approved, and the Data Use Agreement is fully signed.

You can now log in at https://dataportal.answerals.org/datasets and see the data on the Datasets tab. Small packages such as clinical data and metadata download in the browser. Large omics packages use the download tool, which you will find under "How to download Big Data?" on the Download Data page. The Learning Center has short videos for both: https://dataportal.answerals.org/learning-center

Access is on your account only. Please download the data once and keep it on shared storage for the people named on your agreement, rather than having each person download it again.

If you need to add or remove a collaborator later, reply to this email and we will update the agreement.

[Sender name]
[Program] data team, data@answerals.org

## Fill in

- [First name]: the requester's first name.
- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI, as on the request.
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.
