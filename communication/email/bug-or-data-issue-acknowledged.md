# Bug or data issue acknowledged

## When to use

A user reports a portal error, a download failure, a broken link or a value in the data that looks wrong.
Send it to say the report reached us and what happens next, before the fix is known.

## Subject

Re: [Their subject line]

## Body

Hi [First name],

Thank you for letting us know about [The problem]. We have it, and [Who is looking] is looking into it now.

[What to do meanwhile]

[One ask]

We will write back by [Reply-by date] with what we found, even if the fix is not ready yet.

[Sender name]
[Program] data team, data@answerals.org

## Fill in

- [Their subject line]: the subject of the email you are answering.
- [First name]: the user's first name.
- [The problem]: a few words in the user's terms, such as "the 403 error on the Datasets tab".
- [Who is looking]: a team or role, such as "our portal team", never a person's name.
- [What to do meanwhile]: one safe workaround from the Lou Downloading and CLI guide, such as "If the download stopped, reopen the download tool and select the same saved result; it skips files that are already complete." Delete the line if there is none.
- [One ask]: one thing you need from them, such as "Could you send the log file from the Issue Log in the download tool?" Delete it if you need nothing. Never ask for participant identifiers.
- [Reply-by date]: today plus [Standard bug reply time].
- [Standard bug reply time]: the team's usual time to a first real answer. Not settled; ask the program lead.
- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI.
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.

If the report could mean identifiable information is in a file, do not reply with details. Escalate to [Privacy escalation role] the same day, as communication/README.md says.
