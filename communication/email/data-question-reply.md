# Data question reply

## When to use

A user asks what data exists, how a field is defined, how many participants have a data type, or how to find or download a file.
Answer from the Lou pages (lou/INDEX.md), the data pages in data/, the release notes and the README; if the answer is not there, say so and route the question.

## Subject

Re: [Their subject line]

## Body

Hi [First name],

Thanks for your question about [Topic].

[The answer, with its source]

[Where to look next]

[What is still open]

[Sender name]
[Program] data team, data@answerals.org

## Fill in

- [Their subject line]: the subject of the email you are answering.
- [First name]: the user's first name.
- [Topic]: a few words, such as "iPSC lines" or "the download tool".
- [The answer, with its source]: two or three sentences that answer the question and name the file, field or page it comes from. Counts come only as quoted from a Lou page, at release level.
- [Where to look next]: one line with one public link from answer-als/links/link-registry.csv. Never a SAS link.
- [What is still open]: only when part of the question has no answer here. Example: "We do not have a published number for that overlap, so I have passed it to the team and will write back when I have it." Otherwise delete the line.
- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI.
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.

## Common asks

- How do I get access: the DUA request on the Datasets tab, Data Access Committee review, then DocuSign. Use the access templates.
- How many participants have a data type: quote a Lou page (lou/INDEX.md). No new counts, no counts below release level.
- Where is the download tool, is there a Python package: the tool is on the Download Data page under "How to download Big Data?". There is no Python package and no API.
- Can a collaborator get separate access: no. The team downloads once and keeps one shared copy for everyone on the agreement.
- Can I buy an iPSC line: lines are sold by the Cedars-Sinai Biomanufacturing Center; point to its online catalog by name.
- Is miRNA or small RNA sequencing available: no.
- OMOP files: in the clinical package, standardized to OMOP CDM v5.4. The scripts are at https://github.com/Answer-ALS-Data/standardized-omop
- Where are the training videos: the Learning Center, https://dataportal.answerals.org/learning-center
