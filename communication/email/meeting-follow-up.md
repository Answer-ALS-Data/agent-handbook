# Meeting follow-up

## When to use

After a call or meeting with a user, partner or colleague, to confirm what was agreed and who does what next.
Send it to everyone who attended, within [Follow-up window] of the meeting.

## Subject

Follow-up: [Meeting name], [Meeting date]

## Body

Hi [First name or all],

Thank you for the time on [Meeting date]. Here is what we agreed, so we are all working from the same list.

Decided:

- [Decision]

Next steps:

- [Owner]: [Action], by [Due date]

[Open question]

If I missed or misheard anything, reply and I will correct it.

[Sender name]
[Program] data team

## Fill in

- [Follow-up window]: how soon the team sends follow-ups. Not settled; ask the program lead.
- [Meeting name]: a short name, such as "ALS TDI Release 2 planning".
- [Meeting date]: written as October 20, 2026.
- [First name or all]: one first name, or "all" for a group.
- [Decision]: one line per decision. Repeat the bullet as needed.
- [Owner]: the person or role who takes the action. Use roles for anyone not in the meeting.
- [Action]: the action, starting with a verb. Repeat the bullet per action.
- [Due date]: when it is due.
- [Open question]: one open question and who will answer it. Delete the line if nothing is open.
- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI.
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.
