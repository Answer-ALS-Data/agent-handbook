# Access request needs information

## When to use

The team screened a request and something is missing before it can go to the Data Access Committee.
Ask for one missing item per email; if more are missing, ask for the most blocking one first.

## Subject

One more detail for your Neuromine data access request

## Body

Hi [First name],

Thank you for your data access request for [Program]. Before we can send it to the Data Access Committee, we need one thing from you.

[Missing item, in one plain sentence]

You can update the request on the Datasets tab at https://dataportal.answerals.org/datasets, or reply to this email with the details. We review requests [Review cadence], so it will move on soon after we hear back.

[Sender name]
[Program] data team, data@answerals.org

## Fill in

- [First name]: the requester's first name.
- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI, as on the request.
- [Missing item, in one plain sentence]: what is missing and what a good answer looks like. Example: "Please add an Authorized Business Official, a named person at your institution who can sign contracts. A shared office address cannot be accepted." Common gaps: a Principal Investigator at PI, Director or equivalent level; a Business Official who is a different person from the PI; a data use proposal that says what the data is for; a collaborator from another institution, who needs that institution's own request.
- [Review cadence]: how often the team reviews requests. The Lou training documents tell users "three times a week"; confirm before use.
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.
