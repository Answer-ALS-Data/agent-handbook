# Partner outreach

## When to use

A first note to a lab, institute, company or consortium about sharing data, using Neuromine data or working together.
It goes from a team member's own address, and the program lead sees the draft before it is sent.

## Subject

[Program] and [Partner organization]: [Idea in a few words]

## Body

Hi [First name],

I work on the [Program] data team, which publishes [Program] data on the Neuromine Data Portal for approved researchers. [How you know of them]

[The proposal]

Would you be open to a 30 minute call in the next few weeks to talk it through? [Proposed times]

[Sender name]
[Program] data team

## Fill in

- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI.
- [Partner organization]: the organization's name as it writes it.
- [Idea in a few words]: for example "shared splicing data".
- [First name]: the contact's first name.
- [How you know of them]: one real, checkable reason, such as "I read your recent paper on motor neuron splicing." Delete it if there is none.
- [The proposal]: two or three sentences on the one thing you propose and why it helps them. Facts about our data come from the programs/ pages only.
- [Proposed times]: two or three slots, or "I can work around your calendar."
- [Sender name]: the first name of the person sending.
