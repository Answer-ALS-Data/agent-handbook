# Program update outline

A 10 to 15 minute update on one program for funders, partners or the wider team: what changed, the numbers, what is next and how to get access. Nine slides, about 90 seconds each.

Layouts are the deck layouts in design/README.md: Title slide (brand blue, white logo, eyebrow, headline, subline), Content slide (white, blue strip, eyebrow and headline on the left, one chart or screenshot on the right) and the closing slide, which uses the Title slide pattern. Headlines state the point in one sentence. Every number comes from the programs/ pages or the release notes, at release level, with a source line naming the release.

## Slide 1. Title slide

- Eyebrow: [Program] update
- Headline: [Program] Release [Release number] is [live or staged]
- On it: subline with the speaker, the team and [Talk date].

## Slide 2. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Where we are
- Headline: [One sentence on the state of the program today]
- On it: a short timeline of the last three releases, the current one marked in Brand.

## Slide 3. Content slide

- Eyebrow: What changed
- Headline: [The biggest change in the release, as a sentence]
- On it: the three changes from the release notes, one line each, in the release notes' own words.

## Slide 4. Content slide

- Eyebrow: By the numbers
- Headline: [Participant count] participants in the Release [Release number] index
- On it: one bar chart of the release-level counts on the programs/ page (index, clinical, and one omics count), single series in Brand, source line "Source: [Program] Release [Release number] index".

## Slide 5. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Data types
- Headline: [One sentence on which data types grew]
- On it: a small table of data types with the counts the programs/ page or the release notes give; leave a cell blank where neither gives one.

## Slide 6. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Who uses it
- Headline: [One sentence on use, from a published figure only]
- On it: [Usage figure] with its source, or a screenshot of the Neuromine datasets page if no published figure exists.

## Slide 7. Content slide

- Eyebrow: What is next
- Headline: [The next milestone and when]
- On it: two or three planned items, each with an owner role and a month.

## Slide 8. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Getting access
- Headline: Access takes a request, a review and one signature round
- On it: the four steps (request on the Datasets tab, Data Access Committee review, DocuSign, data on the account) and the line "Questions: data@answerals.org".

## Slide 9. Closing slide (Title slide pattern)

- Eyebrow: Thank you
- Headline: [One line the audience should remember]
- On it: subline with https://dataportal.answerals.org and data@answerals.org.

## Fill in

- [Program]: Answer ALS or ALS TDI.
- [Release number]: the whole release number, such as 8 or 2.
- [live or staged]: "live" only after the data owner confirms the release is public.
- [Talk date]: the date of the talk.
- [One sentence on the state of the program today], [The biggest change in the release, as a sentence], [One sentence on which data types grew], [The next milestone and when], [One line the audience should remember]: headlines written for this talk.
- [Participant count]: the index count from the programs/ page for that release.
- [One sentence on use, from a published figure only], [Usage figure]: a usage number the team has published, such as approved users. None is in the handbook yet; ask the program lead.
