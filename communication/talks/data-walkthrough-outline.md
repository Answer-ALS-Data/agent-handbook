# Data walkthrough outline

A 20 minute walkthrough of the data for new users, such as a newly approved lab or an office hours session. Twelve slides, about 90 seconds each, with time for questions at the end.

Layouts are the deck layouts in design/README.md: Title slide, Content slide and the closing slide, which uses the Title slide pattern. On a Content slide the right half holds one screenshot of the portal or one chart, framed with a thin Rule line. Use public screenshots only: no participant rows, no IDs, no download tool screens that show a storage path.

## Slide 1. Title slide

- Eyebrow: Data walkthrough
- Headline: Finding your way around [Program] data
- On it: subline with the speaker, the team and [Talk date].

## Slide 2. Content slide

- Eyebrow: The program
- Headline: [One sentence on what the program is, from programs/]
- On it: who collected the data and how (in clinic for Answer ALS, mostly at home through surveys for ALS TDI).

## Slide 3. Content slide

- Eyebrow: The portal
- Headline: Neuromine is where both programs are published
- On it: screenshot of the Neuromine home page, with the Datasets tab and the Learning Center marked.

## Slide 4. Content slide

- Eyebrow: What is in a release
- Headline: Each release is an index, clinical data, omics and documents
- On it: the parts of the release as listed on the programs/ page, one line each.

## Slide 5. Content slide

- Eyebrow: The index
- Headline: The index has one row per participant
- On it: screenshot of the data table on the portal with the filter panel, and the line "the portal data table and the index hold the same content".

## Slide 6. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Clinical data
- Headline: The dictionary explains every clinical field
- On it: the clinical forms and the data dictionary, and how dates are counted as the release notes describe them (for Answer ALS, days from the screening visit).

## Slide 7. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Omics
- Headline: The sample mapping file ties each sample to a participant
- On it: the data types available and the sample mapping columns from programs/, names only.

## Slide 8. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Finding a cohort
- Headline: Filter first, then save the result
- On it: screenshot of the Data Search filters with one example filter, such as subject group ALS.

## Slide 9. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Downloading
- Headline: Small files download in the browser, large ones need the tool
- On it: the Download Data page, the "How to download Big Data?" section and the line "there is no Python package or API".

## Slide 10. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Working as a team
- Headline: One download, shared with everyone on the agreement
- On it: the rules users ask about most: download once to shared storage, no sharing outside the agreement, a new institution needs its own agreement.

## Slide 11. Content slide

- Eyebrow: Getting help
- Headline: Most answers are in the Learning Center or one email away
- On it: the Learning Center videos, the Lou chatbot on the portal and data@answerals.org.

## Slide 12. Closing slide (Title slide pattern)

- Eyebrow: Questions
- Headline: What would you like to look at together?
- On it: subline with https://dataportal.answerals.org/learning-center and data@answerals.org.
